Getting a good estimate

  • A side view of the whole body works best. Age is judged from the neck, belly, back and legs.
  • If you know the species, pick it instead of Auto. Your pick is always used.
  • Night trail-camera photos work, but black-and-white images give less to go on.

Scoring antlers

  • From a photo gives a field-judge estimate. It's most accurate when both ears show, since they're used as a ruler.
  • Tape measure calculates the score exactly. Enter inches to the nearest eighth. Gross is the total size; net subtracts side-to-side differences and abnormal points, the way the Boone and Crockett record book scores typical racks.
