Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

AgeRack is an app for estimating the age of deer and elk and scoring their antlers. This policy explains what happens to the information you use in the app. AgeRack is made by Jessie Taylor (“we”). Questions go to dirtandfaith78@gmail.com.

The short version: AgeRack has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. You never create an account or give us your name or email. Photos you estimate are sent to an AI service to be analyzed and aren't kept by us. Your history stays on your phone. If you choose to share a scored rack photo, or a harvested animal’s photo with its confirmed age, we keep that photo and those details to improve the app, and you can remove it at any time.

Photos you estimate

When you tap Estimate age or Estimate score, the app sends that photo to our server, which passes it to OpenAI's image analysis service to get the estimate. The photo is sent along with the species you selected. We don't store these photos. OpenAI may keep requests for up to 30 days to monitor for abuse, then deletes them, and doesn't use them to train its models. See OpenAI's privacy policy.

Location data is never sent. The app removes location and camera details from photos before sending them.

Your history

Estimates, scored racks, photos and the names you give animals are saved on your phone only. We can't see them. Deleting an entry, or the app, removes them from your phone. They may be included in your own iCloud or device backups, which Apple handles.

Rack photos you choose to share

When you save a tape-measured score, you can turn on Share to improve photo estimates. It's off unless you turn it on. If you do, we receive and keep:

  • the rack photo, with location and camera details removed
  • the species, your tape measurements and the gross and net scores
  • the anonymous app ID described below, so you can remove what you shared

We use these only to test and improve the accuracy of AgeRack's photo estimates. They are stored privately with our hosting provider and are not published, sold or used for advertising.

To remove a shared photo, press and hold it in History and choose Stop sharing, or delete it and choose Delete and stop sharing. Both delete our copy right away. You can also email us.

Confirmed ages you choose to share

After a harvest, you can enter an animal’s confirmed age (from the jawbone, a lab, or because you’ve known it since it was a fawn) and turn on Share to improve age estimates. It’s off unless you turn it on. If you do, we receive and keep:

  • the photo, with location and camera details removed
  • the species, the confirmed age and how it was found, and the app’s own estimate for that photo
  • the broad National Deer Association region of the state you picked (for example Midwest) and the month the photo was saved, but not your state, spot names or the exact date
  • the anonymous app ID described below, so you can remove what you shared

We use these only to test and improve the accuracy of AgeRack’s age estimates, including training our own estimate model. They are stored privately with our hosting provider and are not published, sold or used for advertising. To remove one, press and hold the photo in History and choose Stop sharing confirmed age, or delete the photo; either deletes our copy right away.

Anonymous app ID

To protect our server from misuse, the app signs in anonymously the first time you use an estimate. This creates a random ID that isn't connected to your name, email, phone number or Apple account. We use it only to allow estimate requests, to count the free age estimates you've used this season, and to connect shared photos to the person who can remove them.

AgeRack Pro subscriptions

Without a subscription you get 10 free age estimates each season (July 1 to June 30). We keep a count of how many you've used, tied to the anonymous app ID, and nothing else about them.

AgeRack Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. With each estimate, the app sends Apple's signed record of your subscription (which plan, its transaction number and when it renews or ends) so our server can confirm it's active. We check it and don't store it. You manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings.

Camera and photo library

The app asks for camera access only when you choose Take photo. When you import a photo, you pick it with Apple's photo picker, so the app only sees the photos you choose.

Service providers

  • Supabase runs our server and stores shared rack photos and confirmed-age photos.
  • OpenAI analyzes photos to produce estimates.

We don't share information with anyone else, except if required by law.

Children

AgeRack isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can use AgeRack without sharing anything beyond the photos you estimate. You can remove shared photos at any time in the app. To ask what we hold, or to have it deleted, email dirtandfaith78@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. If a change affects photos you've already shared, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
